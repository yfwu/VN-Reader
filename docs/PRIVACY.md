# VN Reader 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

Effective for build 7 (iCloud test build), 2026-11-10

## 繁體中文

VN Reader 可完全離線閱讀本機漫畫，不需要 VN Reader 帳號。你可自行選擇開啟 iCloud 同步；關閉時不會把書庫傳送到雲端。

* **不蒐集資料**：App 沒有廣告、分析或追蹤工具，開發者不設置書庫伺服器。你主動啟用的 iCloud 同步與分享功能會傳送所需資料。
* **本機儲存**：本機漫畫、閱讀進度、書籤和書籍資訊存在 App 自己的儲存空間。刪除 App 會移除其本機資料，不會自動刪除已同步到 iCloud 的書庫。
* **選用 iCloud 同步**：開啟後，漫畫原始檔、封面、書籍資訊、閱讀進度與書籤會儲存在你目前 iCloud 帳號的 CloudKit 私有資料庫，並佔用你的 iCloud 空間。開啟同步、啟用後匯入或手動同步會排入原始檔上傳；漫畫傳輸不使用行動數據。新裝置預設只取得資訊、封面與頁面清單，開啟時才下載漫畫。關閉同步會停止同步，但保留本機與雲端資料。此測試版不提供雲端永久刪除；移除本機下載不會刪除雲端原始檔。Apple 處理 iCloud 帳號及雲端服務，適用 Apple 的服務條款與隱私權政策。
* **你主動分享的頁面**：按下分享會把選定頁面交給你選擇的 App 或服務，並適用其隱私權政策。選擇「儲存影像」時，App 會請求將照片加入照片圖庫的權限，但不會讀取你的照片；存入的頁面可能隨 iCloud 照片同步，刪除 App 也不會移除。
* **TestFlight 測試版**：Apple 的 TestFlight 會把當機報告、使用次數和裝置型號等資料提供給開發者；你主動送出的意見回饋與截圖也一樣。這些資料由 Apple 處理，適用 Apple 的 TestFlight 條款，只用來改善 App。
* **與 App 的其他互動**：透過 iCloud 雲碟等檔案服務匯入時，檔案由該服務提供，適用其隱私權政策。本版不包含 WebDAV。

有問題請到 <https://github.com/yfwu/VN-Reader/issues> 提出。政策若有變更，會更新在這個頁面。

## English

VN Reader can read local comics completely offline without a VN Reader account. You may choose to enable iCloud sync; when it is off, the library is not uploaded.

* **No data collection**: the app contains no ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer operates no library server. Sync and sharing transmit the data needed for those features only when you choose to use them.
* **Local storage**: local comics, reading progress, bookmarks and book details are stored in the app's storage. Deleting the app removes its local data but does not automatically delete the library already synced to iCloud.
* **Optional iCloud sync**: when enabled, comic archives, covers, book details, reading progress and bookmarks are stored in the CloudKit private database of your current iCloud account and use your iCloud storage quota. Enabling sync, importing while enabled or requesting Sync Now queues local archives for upload; archive transfers disallow cellular access. New devices initially receive details, covers and page manifests; archives download when opened. Turning sync off preserves local and cloud data. This test build does not offer permanent cloud deletion; removing a local download retains the cloud original. Apple handles iCloud accounts and cloud services under its terms and privacy policy.
* **Pages you share**: tapping Share sends the selected page to the app or service you choose, under its own privacy policy. If you choose Save Image, the app asks for permission to add to your photo library, but it never reads your photos. Saved pages may sync with iCloud Photos and stay there if you delete the app.
* **TestFlight builds**: Apple's TestFlight gives the developer crash reports, session counts and device models, as well as any feedback and screenshots you choose to send. Apple processes this data under its TestFlight terms, and it is used only to improve the app.
* **Other services**: files imported from iCloud Drive or another file provider come from that service, under its own privacy policy. This build does not include WebDAV.

Questions are welcome at <https://github.com/yfwu/VN-Reader/issues>. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
