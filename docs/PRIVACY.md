# VN Reader 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-06

## 繁體中文

VN Reader 是離線的漫畫閱讀器，不需要帳號，也不會連線到任何伺服器。

* **不蒐集資料**：App 不蒐集、不傳送、不分享任何個人資料，也沒有廣告、分析或追蹤工具。
* **你的檔案留在裝置上**：匯入的漫畫、閱讀進度、書籤和書籍資訊都只存在 App 自己的儲存空間。刪除 App 就會一併刪除這些資料。
* **TestFlight 測試版**：Apple 的 TestFlight 會把當機報告、使用次數和裝置型號等資料提供給開發者；你主動送出的意見回饋與截圖也一樣。這些資料由 Apple 處理，適用 Apple 的 TestFlight 條款，只用來改善 App。
* **與 App 的其他互動**：透過 iCloud 雲碟等檔案服務匯入時，檔案由該服務提供，適用其隱私權政策。

有問題請到 <https://github.com/yfwu/VN-Reader/issues> 提出。政策若有變更，會更新在這個頁面。

## English

VN Reader is an offline comic reader. It needs no account and does not connect to any server.

* **No data collection**: the app does not collect, transmit or share personal data, and it contains no ads, analytics or tracking.
* **Your files stay on your device**: imported comics, reading progress, bookmarks and book details are stored only in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.
* **TestFlight builds**: Apple's TestFlight gives the developer crash reports, session counts and device models, as well as any feedback and screenshots you choose to send. Apple processes this data under its TestFlight terms, and it is used only to improve the app.
* **Other services**: files imported from iCloud Drive or another file provider come from that service, under its own privacy policy.

Questions are welcome at <https://github.com/yfwu/VN-Reader/issues>. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
